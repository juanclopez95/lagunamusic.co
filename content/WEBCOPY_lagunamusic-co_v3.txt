Different tempos. One point of view.
Always in motion.
Laguna moves between warm, low-slung house and more reflective electronic forms. The through line is atmosphere: music that changes the room without crowding it.
Recent filmed sessions extend that instinct beyond the booth — long-form selections built around place, light and the pace of a night unfolding.
